# Design — Shveytsariya iqtisodiyoti

Swiss International Style on top of the Japan deck's mechanics.

- **Palette** (`src/index.css`): glacier paper `#f6f7f9`, wash `#eef0f2`, mist `#dfe2e6`, feather `#a9afb8`, dilute `#5d646e`, ink `#23272e`, depth `#131518`, sumi `#08090b`, and one **Swiss red** `#d52b1e` (token `seal`) used for the mark, the active rail item, the "now" marker and the one thing each screen points at.
- **Type:** Inter Tight (grotesk, 200–700) for everything; Instrument Serif italic (`.accent`) for words in the four national languages.
- **Mark:** the Swiss cross on a red rounded square (`components/Seal.tsx`), also the favicon.
- **Motion:** unchanged from the Japan build — word rise, settle-in reveals, pinned ladder, masked whiteout, parallax, flip cards, marquee, shader finale. Colours re-dyed: red/graphite drops in the hero fluid, red currents in the flow field, red hot threads in the mesh.
