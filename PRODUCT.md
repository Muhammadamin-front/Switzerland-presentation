# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + shadcn project structure. Built by re-theming the Japan economy deck (same components from the user's `Prompt` folder) for Switzerland.

## Users

- **Presenter:** a student giving a 10–15 minute talk on the Swiss economy in an economics class, driving the page live with keys or a clicker.
- **Audience:** classmates and the teacher reading big numbers and headlines on a projected screen.

## Product Purpose

A scroll-driven presentation about Switzerland's economy: geography and the "no resources" paradox, key figures, history (1291 → 2026), the franc as a safe haven, Swiss Made industries (pharma, watches, banking, food/machinery), innovation as the climax, problems (ageing, US tariffs, strong franc) and the outlook.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: **Uzbek (Latin script)**. German, French, Italian and Romansh words appear only as accents.
- Every figure is sourced and dated (IMF WEO Oct 2026, BAZG, FH, SECO, FSO, SNB, WIPO, EPO, SBA); approximations carry "~".
- Requires internet for the Spline scene and Google Fonts.

## Product Principles

1. One idea per screen, big numbers, short sentences.
2. Every figure has a source and a year.
3. Innovation is the climax — tied to economics (strong franc, wages, exports), not just "cool tech".
4. Reliable on a laptop during a live talk; `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
